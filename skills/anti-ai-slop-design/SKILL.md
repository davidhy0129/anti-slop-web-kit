# Anti-AI-Slop Design Engineering

Use this skill to make any web interface look intentionally crafted by a senior digital product studio rather than generated by an LLM template.

## 1. Typography: Avoid AI Clichés

### Forbidden Default AI Fonts
Do **not** use these unless an existing brand identity explicitly mandates them:
- **Sans/Display clichés:** `Inter`, `Roboto`, `Arial`, `Geist`, `Space Grotesk`, `Bricolage Grotesque`, `Instrument Sans`, `Plus Jakarta` overuse with default Tailwind tracking
- **Serif clichés:** `Instrument Serif`, `Fraunces`, `Playfair Display`
- **Monospace overuse:** `JetBrains Mono`, `Space Mono` (never use monospace for marketing eyebrows, badges, or fake terminal vibes)

### Approved High-Craft Alternatives
Choose **at most 2 fonts** (often 1 strong sans family is cleaner than 2):
- **Clean / Swiss / Engineering / ERP:** `Switzer`, `Saans`, `ABC Diatype`, `TWK Lausanne`, `General Sans`
- **Modern / Friendly / Hospitality / SaaS:** `Aeonik`, `PP Mori`, `Circular`, `GT Walsheim`, `Studio Feixen Sans`, `Degular`, `Poppins`, `DM Sans`, `Satoshi`
- **Distinctive Editorial / Character:** `Familjen Grotesk` (free on Google Fonts), `Aktiv Grotesk`, `Apfel Grotezk`, `ABC Favorit`

### Typographic Precision Rules
- **Display headings (`H1`, `H2`):** Tight tracking (`tracking-[-0.03em]` or `-0.025em`) and tight leading (`leading-[1.08]` to `1.15`).
- **No trailing periods** on `H1` or `H2` marketing headings (e.g., `"Built for speed"` not `"Built for speed."`).
- **Tabular numbers:** Always apply `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` (`tabular-nums` in Tailwind) on prices, metrics, clocks, tables, and KPI counters so digits never jitter.
- **Line length:** Cap prose paragraphs at `55ch–68ch` (`max-w-prose`) for effortless scanning.

## 2. The 3 Golden Rules of Color

Most AI-generated sites fail visually because they combine clinical cool white/gray (`#FFFFFF` + `#EEF2F6`) with 4–5 competing accent badges (blue, green, yellow, purple, red).

### Rule 1: Maximum 3 Core Colors
1. **1 Dark Base** (Navbar, high-contrast bands, primary text, footer)
2. **1 Soulful Light Base** (Warm stone/bone/cream off-white + crisp `#FFFFFF` surface cards — far more human than clinical `#F1F5F9` blue-gray)
3. **1 Single Protagonist Accent** (Reserved **exclusively** for what you are selling: primary CTAs, active tabs, and key conversion points)

### Rule 2: Warm-vs-Cool Temperature Contrast
*Cool page = warm accent. Warm page = cool accent.*
When background, dark surface, and accent are all cool (navy + cool gray + cyan + green), the page feels flat. Crossing temperature creates instant visual hierarchy.

#### Proven 3-Color Palettes (Ready to Use)
| Mood / Industry | Dark Base | Light Base | Protagonist Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Executive / Architecture / Consulting** | `#0B1325` (Deep Indigo) | `#F6F5F1` (Warm Bone) + `#A9CDBE` (Celadon tint) | `#F2B53A` (Warm Gold) |
| **Energy / Tech / Modern Services** | `#0B1325` (Deep Navy) | `#FAFAF8` (Warm Paper) + `#F0EFEA` (Stone) | `#00C875` (Electric Green) or `#FF6A1F` (Signal Orange) |
| **Hospitality / Restaurant / Lifestyle** | `#0F372B` (Bottle Green) | `#EFE7DA` (Warm Bone) | `#E8392B` (Vermilion) |
| **Craft / Studio / Creative** | `#013330` (Deep Teal) | `#F3EBDD` (Cream) | `#FF6A1F` (Warm Orange) |
| **Bold SaaS / Fintech / Sports Club** | `#0C1B48` (Midnight Navy) | `#F5F7FA` + `#52A7EA` (Glacier tint) | `#F2EA1E` (High-Contrast Lemon) |
| **Minimal Monochrome + Editorial** | `#141413` (Charcoal) | `#F7F6F2` (Alabaster) | `#D94E28` (Terracotta) |

### Rule 3: Reserve the Accent for Action
- Do **not** paint decorative icons, eyebrow pills, or secondary tags in the protagonist accent.
- Avoid gratuitous linear gradients on backgrounds and buttons unless part of an explicit brand mark; solid, confident surfaces age better and feel more authentic.

## 3. Purge the "AI-Slop" Indicators

Audit every component and remove these dead giveaways of AI-generated UI:
- **Uppercase Pill Eyebrows Above Every Heading:** Delete stacks of `[ TECHNICAL TRANSPARENCY ]`, `[ FEATURE 01 ]`, `[ WHY CHOOSE US ]` pills above titles. Let strong typography and spacing create hierarchy.
- **Fake Step Numbering (`01`, `02`, `03`):** Remove numeric prefixes on cards unless the user literally must complete steps in sequence.
- **Excessive Card Borders & Hairline Dividers:** Replace `1px solid` boxes around every element with clean whitespace, subtle surface contrast (`#F6F5F1` vs `#FFFFFF`), or soft elevation.
- **Icon-in-Rounded-Square 3-Column Clutter:** Do not default to 3 identical cards each with a Lucide icon in a tinted `40x40` square + bold title + 3-line paragraph. Vary visual weight and show real product UI, photography, or concise data instead.
- **Concentric Border Radii:** Whenever a rounded card contains a rounded image or inner box, enforce:
  $$\text{innerRadius} = \max(0,\; \text{outerRadius} - \text{padding})$$
  *(Example: outer card `rounded-3xl` (`24px`) with `p-3` (`12px`) padding → inner photo must be `rounded-xl` (`12px`), never `rounded-3xl`.)*
- **White-Background Logos/Images:** On light surfaces, apply `mix-blend-multiply` so white-box JPEGs/PNGs melt seamlessly into the card background; never invent fake brand logos or SVGs when real logos exist.
- **Marquee / Carousel Edge Masks:** Always fade horizontal logo strips or carousels on both edges using CSS `mask-image: linear-gradient(to right, transparent, black 12%, black 88%, transparent)`.
- **Zero Fake Social Proof:** Never invent testimonials, star ratings, or fake customer names. If real reviews don't exist yet, omit reviews and highlight verifiable facts, process guarantees, or real case photos.
